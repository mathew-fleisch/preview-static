Heritage & Mission

Our Story

Founded in Novato, California, The Velvet Fog Cannabis Company was born out of a deep appreciation for Northern California's rich cannabis culture and a commitment to providing Marin County residents with a safe, professional, and discreet delivery experience.

As Novato's first licensed cannabis delivery dispensary (State License # C12-0000433-LIC), we recognized the need for a service that prioritizes top-tier product curation, transparent pricing with all taxes included, and prompt, reliable customer care across every corner of Marin.

"Whether you call it cannabis, grass, mota, marijuana, pakalolo, pot or just weed, The Velvet Fog has what you want, delivered straight to your door."

We partner exclusively with California's finest licensed cultivators, extractors, and artisans—ranging from award-winning flower growers to solventless live rosin makers—ensuring that every item on our menu meets our exacting standards of purity, potency, and flavor.

From our drivers equipped with mobile debit card readers to our seamless online ACH payments on sweede.io and dedicated phone support at (415) 940-9849, we are here to guide you to the best cannabis experience possible.
