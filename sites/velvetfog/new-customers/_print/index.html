Onboarding Guide

First-Time Customer 13-Step Onboarding Guide

Ordering cannabis delivery in Novato and Marin County is simple and secure. Follow our complete vertical mobile walkthrough below to register your account, verify your ID, browse our menu, and complete your order on sweede.io.

Open Online Menu Call Us: (415) 940-9849
Step-by-Step

Complete Mobile Checkout Walkthrough

Scroll vertically through each step below.


	
	

		

			
			

				
Step 1: Enter Your Delivery Address

				
Enter the address where you'd like your order delivered within Marin County. Tap Save and Show Menu to continue.

			

		

	


	
	

		

			
			

				
Step 2: Browse the Menu

				
Browse our menu and tap the + button to quickly add products to your cart. When you're ready, tap the Cart button.

			

		

	


	
	

		

			
			

				
Step 3: Review Your Cart

				
Double-check your order items and totals. Tap Go to Checkout to continue.

			

		

	


	
	

		

			
			

				
Step 4: Enter Your Phone Number

				
Enter your mobile phone number. A text message with a secure verification code will be sent to you. Tap Continue.

			

		

	


	
	

		

			
			

				
Step 5: Enter Your Verification Code

				
Enter the 4-digit code received via text message to verify your phone number.

			

		

	


	
	

		

			
			

				
Step 6: Verify Your Identity

				
Tap Verify to begin the required age and identity verification process.

			

		

	


	
	

		

			
			

				
Step 7: Choose Your ID Type

				
Select the type of government-issued photo ID you'd like to use (Driver's License, State ID, or Passport).

			

		

	


	
	

		

			
			

				
Step 8: Upload a Photo of Your ID

				
Choose Use Camera to take a live photo or Upload Photo if you have one saved. Follow the on-screen framing guides.

			

		

	


	
	

		

			
			

				
Step 9: Complete Verification

				
Review your scanned information. If everything looks correct, tap Complete.

			

		

	


	
	

		

			
			

				
Step 10: Verification Complete

				
Your identity has been verified successfully. You'll see this confirmation screen for a moment before returning to your order.

			

		

	


	
	

		

			
			

				
Step 11: Sign the Consent Form

				
Use your finger to sign in the signature box. Tap Submit Consent.

			

		

	


	
	

		

			
			

				
Step 12: Place Your Order

				
Review your order one last time along with delivery details and taxes. When ready, tap Place Order.

			

		

	


	
	

		

			
			

				
Step 13: You're All Set!

				
Your order has been placed successfully. You'll receive live updates as your order is prepared and when your driver is on the way.
