Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question below to expand the answer.


	
	

		
			How old do I have to be to use your service?
		
		

			If you are 21 or over with a valid government issued ID, you can purchase cannabis in the state of California. Individuals who are 18-20 and have acquired a medical recommendation card, as well as a valid government issued ID, are also eligible for our delivery services.
		

	


	
	

		
			Where do you deliver?
		
		

			We deliver all throughout Marin County, spanning Novato down to Southern Marin and West Marin coastal towns.
		

	


	
	

		
			What is the delivery minimum? Is there a daily limit?
		
		

			Minimum order ranges from $50 to $200 depending on your delivery zone. Additionally, the state daily limit for adult-use (non-medical) customers is 1 ounce of cannabis flower and 8 grams of concentrated cannabis.
		

	


	
	

		
			When are you open?
		
		

			We are open 7 days a week from 10:00 AM to 6:55 PM. Note for customers south of San Rafael and Fairfax: orders must be received by 6:40 PM for same-day delivery.
		

	


	
	

		
			Can I schedule for a specific delivery time?
		
		

			Yes! We will do our best to accommodate your preferred delivery window. Let our staff know over the phone or leave a note in the 'Notes' section of your online order.
		

	


	
	

		
			Do you have a storefront I can visit?
		
		

			Unfortunately, no. The city of Novato does not permit walk-in retail cannabis storefronts at this time. We operate exclusively as a licensed delivery service.
		

	


	
	

		
			What forms of payment do you accept?
		
		

			We do NOT accept credit cards. We DO accept cash and PIN debit cards upon delivery. We strongly encourage customers to pay online securely using our simple ACH payment method.
		

	


	
	

		
			Are taxes included in the price?
		
		

			Yes! No surprises at checkout. All state and local taxes are fully included in your final price.
		

	


	
	

		
			Do you accept returns or exchanges for defective items?
		
		

			Due to California state regulations, all sales are final. If you receive a defective item (such as a malfunctioning vape cartridge), call our office immediately at (415) 940-9849 on the same day so we can resolve the issue.
		

	


	
	

		
			The online delivery system says my address is invalid, what do I do?
		
		

			Our system connects with Google Maps and occasionally encounters hiccups with obscure addresses. Please ensure proper abbreviations for Drive (Dr.), Avenue (Ave.), and Street (St.). If the issue persists, call us directly at (415) 940-9849.
		

	







	
California Cannabis Laws FAQs

	
Know your rights and responsibilities under California state law.






	

		
			Where can I publicly use cannabis?
		
		

			Just like tobacco, smoking or vaping cannabis in public spaces, restaurants, and parks is prohibited. Driving under the influence of cannabis is strictly illegal.
		

	


	

		
			Where should I consume cannabis?
		
		

			We highly recommend consuming cannabis only at your private residence or authorized private locations.
		

	


	

		
			Can I grow cannabis at home?
		
		

			Yes! In California, you are legally allowed to grow up to 6 plants per private residence at any given time, provided they are not visible to the public.
		

	


	

		
			Can I sell cannabis to friends?
		
		

			No. Selling cannabis without a state license is illegal. Adults 21+ may gift specified amounts of cannabis to other adults 21+ without any compensation.
		

	


	

		
			How much cannabis can I carry on me?
		
		

			In California, you may legally carry up to one ounce of cannabis and eight grams of concentrated cannabis. Bringing cannabis into federal buildings (courts, military bases, national parks) is prohibited.
		

	


	

		
			Can my employer prevent me from using cannabis?
		
		

			Yes. Employers retain the legal right to enforce drug-free workplace policies and prohibit cannabis use by employees.
		

	


	

		
			Can I travel outside of California with cannabis?
		
		

			No. Transporting cannabis across state lines is a federal offense, even when traveling to another legal state.
		

	


	

		
			Can I keep cannabis in my car?
		
		

			Yes, but ONLY in a sealed package or container stored in your car's trunk. Having an open container or consuming cannabis while driving is strictly illegal.
