A–Z Cannabis Glossary & Terminology

Whether you are a seasoned connoisseur or exploring cannabis for the first time, understanding key terminology helps you make informed choices on our menu.

Common Terms

  • 710: Oil day celebrated on July 10th. Upside down, “710” spells “OIL”, serving as a celebration of cannabis concentrates, extracts, and dabbing.
  • Cannabinoids: Chemical compounds found in the cannabis plant that interact with the human endocannabinoid system (e.g., THC, CBD, CBG, CBN).
  • Live Resin: A type of cannabis concentrate made from freshly harvested flash-frozen cannabis plants, preserving the plant’s delicate terpene profile.
  • Live Rosin: A solventless concentrate extracted using heat and pressure from bubble hash made from flash-frozen material. Highly prized for purity and flavor.
  • Terpenes: Aromatic compounds naturally occurring in cannabis and other plants that give strains their distinct smells and flavors while modulating the entourage effect.

Cannabis Product Types: Flower, Edibles, Concentrates & More

Modern cannabis menus offer an incredible variety of consumption methods. Understanding how each product works ensures a great experience.

Key Formats

  • Flower: Traditional cannabis buds. Fast onset when smoked or vaporized; full entourage effect from natural cannabinoids and terpenes.
  • Edibles: Gummies, baked goods, and beverages. Longer onset (30–90 minutes) and longer duration of effects. Start low and go slow!
  • Cartridges & Vapes: Discreet, convenient, and portable oil cartridges providing instant onset with minimal odor.
  • Extracts & Concentrates: Potent dabs, shatter, and live rosin designed for experienced consumers seeking maximum potency and flavor.
  • Topicals: Lotions and balms applied directly to the skin for localized relief without psychoactive effects.

Rosin vs. Live Rosin vs. Live Resin: Understanding Extracts

Concentrates have evolved rapidly. Here is how to distinguish between live resin, live rosin, and other popular extract types.

Extraction Methods

  • Live Resin: Extracted using solvents (like butane or propane) from freshly harvested, flash-frozen cannabis plants to preserve maximum terpenes.
  • Live Rosin: Completely solventless extraction. Made by washing flash-frozen flower into bubble hash, then applying heat and pressure. The gold standard for purity and flavor.
  • Cured Resin: Similar to live resin, but produced using cured and dried cannabis flower rather than fresh-frozen material.
